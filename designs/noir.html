About Al Farash

Royal interiors, everyday prices

Al Farash Home Decor is based in Manchester and started with one idea: bring the richness of royal interiors into everyday homes, at a fair price.

Our signature is the Greek key, set in soft crushed velvet with gold, silver and crystal detail. Alongside our cushion covers we make curtain and blind sets to measure, plus curtains, table runners, placemat sets and bed throw sets, so a whole room can match.

We have sold on eBay since 2012, with 100% positive feedback from more than 3,700 sales.
