Navy blue & yellow Gold Crushed Velvet Round Greek Key Cushion Cover with Baroque Urn, 18" x 18" (45 x 45 cm)

A luxurious Navy Blue crushed velvet cushion cover with a yellow gold Greek key border and Greek key circle surrounding an ornate baroque urn. Crushed velvet gives the cover a rich, light-catching texture with a soft, luxurious feel. The Greek key, also called the meander pattern, has decorated fine interiors for centuries and gives the cover a refined, timeless look. The yellow gold detail stands out beautifully against the Navy Blue background. The round Greek key circle is the centrepiece of the design.

This 18 inch (45cm) decorative cushion cover, also known as a throw pillow case or scatter cushion cover, adds luxury home decor to sofas, beds, armchairs and window seats. Use it on its own as a statement piece, hotel-style look. It pairs well with other cushions from our Greek key collection, and with our matching curtains and table runners, so you can style a whole room. It also makes a lovely gift for housewarmings, weddings, birthdays and special occasions.
