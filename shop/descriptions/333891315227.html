Red & Silver Crushed Velvet Greek Key Stripe Cushion Cover with Diamante Crystal Stones, 16" x 16" (40 x 40 cm)

A beautiful red crushed velvet cushion cover with a Greek key band of sparkling silver crystal stones running vertically down the middle. The crushed velvet has a textured, shimmering finish that catches the light from every angle and feels soft to the touch. The Greek key (also called the meander pattern) comes from ancient Greek design. It never goes out of style and gives the cover a refined, timeless look. The diamante crystal stones catch the light and add a touch of glamour. The middle Greek key stripe runs right across the cover.

This 16 inch (40cm) decorative cushion cover, also known as a throw pillow case or scatter cushion cover, adds luxury home decor to sofas, beds and accent chairs. Use it on its own as a statement piece, or layer it with plain silver or red cushions for a coordinated, hotel-style look. It pairs well with other cushions from our Greek key collection, and with our matching curtains and table runners, so you can style a whole room. It also makes a lovely gift for housewarmings, weddings, birthdays and special occasions.
