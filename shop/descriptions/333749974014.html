Navy Blue & Gold Velvet Greek Key Scorpio Zodiac Cushion Cover, 17" x 17" (43 x 43 cm)

A striking navy blue velvet cushion cover with a gold Greek key border framing a detailed scorpion for the Scorpio zodiac sign. The soft, plush velvet has a gentle sheen that shifts with the light, giving the cover a rich, luxurious look and a lovely feel. The Greek key (also called the meander pattern) comes from ancient Greek design. It never goes out of style and gives the cover a refined, timeless look. The gold detail stands out beautifully against the navy blue background.

This 17 inch (43cm) decorative cushion cover, also known as a throw pillow case or scatter cushion cover, adds luxury home decor to sofas, beds, armchairs and window seats. Use it on its own as a statement piece, or layer it with plain gold or navy blue cushions for a coordinated, hotel-style look. It pairs well with other cushions from our Greek key collection, and with our matching curtains and table runners, so you can style a whole room. It also makes a lovely gift for housewarmings, weddings, birthdays and special occasions.
