Black & Gold Crushed Velvet Large Greek Key Cushion Cover, 24" x 24" (60 x 60cm)

A striking Black crushed velvet cushion cover with a large Gold panel cut with four oversized Greek key spirals. The crushed velvet has a textured, shimmering finish that catches the light from every angle and feels soft to the touch. The Greek key (also called the meander pattern) comes from ancient Greek design. It never goes out of style and gives the cover a refined, timeless look. The gold detail stands out beautifully against the grey background.

This 24”inch (60cm) decorative cushion cover, also known as a throw pillow case or scatter cushion cover, adds luxury home decor to sofas, beds and accent chairs. Use it on its own as a statement piece, or layer it with plain Gold or Black cushions for a coordinated, hotel-style look. It pairs well with other cushions from our Greek key collection, and with our matching curtains and table runners, so you can style a whole room. It also makes a lovely gift for housewarmings, weddings, birthdays and special occasions.
