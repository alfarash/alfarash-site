Ivory & Silver Crushed Velvet Greek Keys Border Cushion Cover with Greek Keys Scribbles Centre, 17" x 17" (43 x 43 cm)

A beautiful ivory crushed velvet cushion cover with a silver Greek key border framing a square Greek key motif at the centre. The crushed velvet has a textured, shimmering finish that catches the light from every angle and feels soft to the touch. The Greek key (also called the meander pattern) comes from ancient Greek design. It never goes out of style and gives the cover a refined, timeless look. The silver detail stands out beautifully against the ivory background.

This 17 inch (43cm) decorative cushion cover, also known as a throw pillow case or scatter cushion cover, adds luxury home decor to sofas, beds, armchairs and window seats. Use it on its own as a statement piece, or layer it with plain silver or ivory cushions for a coordinated, hotel-style look. It pairs well with other cushions from our Greek key collection, and with our matching curtains and table runners, so you can style a whole room. It also makes a lovely gift for housewarmings, weddings, birthdays and special occasions.
