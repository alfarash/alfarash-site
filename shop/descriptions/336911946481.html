Product Description

The smooth, subtly lustre surface of the set adds a luxurious, premium feel, ideal for everyday dining, dinner parties, special occasions, family gatherings and festive celebrations. The intricate rose design brings a classic, romantic decorative touch, complementing traditional, classic and modern‑luxury dining room interiors.

Practical as well as stylish, these table mats, runner and coasters help protect your table surface from spills, heat and scratches. Easy to wipe clean and maintain, this stunning matching dining set offers a simple yet impactful way to elevate your table presentation and impress your guests.
