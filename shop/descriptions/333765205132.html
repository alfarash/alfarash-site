Black & Gold Velvet Round Greek Key Cushion Cover with Diamante Crystal Stones, 24" x 24" (60 x 60 cm)

A beautiful black velvet cushion cover with a gold Greek key border and concentric Greek key circles with crystal stones at the centre. The soft, plush velvet has a gentle sheen that shifts with the light, giving the cover a rich, luxurious look and a lovely feel. The Greek key (also called the meander pattern) comes from ancient Greek design. It never goes out of style and gives the cover a refined, timeless look. The diamante crystal stones catch the light and add a touch of glamour. The round Greek key circle is the centrepiece of the design.

This 24 inch (60cm) decorative cushion cover, also known as a throw pillow case or scatter cushion cover, is a large statement piece, ideal for big sofas, beds and floor seating. It pairs well with other cushions from our Greek key collection, and with our matching curtains and table runners, so you can style a whole room. It also makes a lovely gift for housewarmings, weddings, birthdays and special occasions.
