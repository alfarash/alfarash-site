Grey & Silver Velvet Greek Key Border Cushion Cover with Greek Key Centre, 24" x 24" (60 x 60cm)

An elegant grey velvet cushion cover with a silver Greek key border surrounding a square Greek key motif at the centre. Made from soft, plush velvet with a subtle sheen, it feels as good as it looks and adds instant warmth to a room. The Greek key, also called the meander pattern, has decorated fine interiors for centuries and gives the cover a refined, timeless look. The silver detail stands out beautifully against the grey background.

This 24 inch (60cm) decorative cushion cover, also known as a throw pillow case or scatter cushion cover, adds luxury home decor to sofas, beds, armchairs and window seats. Use it on its own as a statement piece, or layer it with plain silver or grey cushions for a coordinated, hotel-style look. It pairs well with other cushions from our Greek key collection, and with our matching curtains and table runners, so you can style a whole room. It also makes a lovely gift for housewarmings, weddings, birthdays and special occasions.
