Red & Gold Crushed Velvet Side Greek Key Cushion Cover with Diamante Crystal Stones, 16" x 16" (40 x 40 cm)

A luxurious red crushed velvet cushion cover with a gold Greek key band of sparkling crystal stones running down one side. Crushed velvet gives the cover a rich, light-catching texture with a soft, luxurious feel. The Greek key, also called the meander pattern, has decorated fine interiors for centuries and gives the cover a refined, timeless look. The diamante crystal stones catch the light and add a touch of glamour.

This 16 inch (40cm) decorative cushion cover, also known as a throw pillow case or scatter cushion cover, adds luxury home decor to living rooms, bedrooms and reading corners. Use it on its own as a statement piece, or layer it with plain gold or red cushions for a coordinated, hotel-style look. It pairs well with other cushions from our Greek key collection, and with our matching curtains and table runners, so you can style a whole room. It also makes a lovely gift for housewarmings, weddings, birthdays and special occasions.
