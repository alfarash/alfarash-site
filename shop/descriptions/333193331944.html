Ivory & Silver Crushed Velvet Middle Greek Key Border Square Cushion Cover, 18" x 18" (45 x 45 cm)

A luxurious ivory crushed velvet cushion cover with a single silver Greek key square of four interlocking spirals set at the centre. Crushed velvet gives the cover a rich, light-catching texture with a soft, luxurious feel. The Greek key, also called the meander pattern, has decorated fine interiors for centuries and gives the cover a refined, timeless look. The silver detail stands out beautifully against the ivory background.

This 18 inch (45cm) decorative cushion cover, also known as a throw pillow case or scatter cushion cover, adds luxury home decor to sofas, beds, armchairs and window seats. Use it on its own as a statement piece, or layer it with plain silver or ivory cushions for a coordinated, hotel-style look. It pairs well with other cushions from our Greek key collection, and with our matching curtains and table runners, so you can style a whole room. It also makes a lovely gift for housewarmings, weddings, birthdays and special occasions.
